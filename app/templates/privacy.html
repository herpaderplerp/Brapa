Brapa ("we", "us") is operated by [OPERATOR / LEGAL ENTITY]. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Who this applies to

Anyone who signs in to or uses Brapa at [SITE URL].

What we collect

  • Account / identity (via Google Sign-In): your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile picture URL. We use OAuth — we never see or store your Google password.
  • Profile you provide: display name, home region, avatar, and unit/notification/discoverability preferences.
  • Ride data you upload: GPX files including precise location tracks, timestamps, elevation and speed, plus derived statistics, the reverse-geocoded start area, and historical weather for the ride's start.
  • Photos you add: the image files and, if present, their embedded EXIF GPS coordinates and capture time (or coordinates you place manually).
  • Social content: bikes in your garage, friendships, likes, and comments.
  • Operational data: standard server logs (IP address, request metadata) for security and debugging.

How we use it

  • Provide the service: process rides, render maps, compute stats, run the feed, social graph, notifications, and discovery.
  • Enforce your privacy choices (ride visibility, profile discoverability).
  • Send the email notifications you opt into.
  • Secure and maintain the service.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Third parties we send data to

  • Google — authentication (Sign-In).
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — we send a ride's start coordinates to reverse-geocode the region.
  • Open-Meteo — we send a ride's start coordinates and time to fetch historical weather.
  • Map tile providers (OpenStreetMap, Esri, OpenTopoMap) — your browser requests map tiles directly when viewing maps.

We do not control these providers' own policies; review them separately.

Location data

GPX tracks reveal where you have been. You control who sees each ride via its visibility setting (Public, Friends only, Private). Private rides are never shown to other users; friends-only rides are restricted to your accepted friends. We enforce this on the server, not just in the interface.

Retention

We keep your data until you delete it or close your account. Deleting a ride removes its track, points, weather, and photos. Deleting your account removes your profile and all associated rides, bikes, photos, comments, likes, and friendships, and purges the stored files.

Your rights and choices

  • Export: download your original GPX files at any time from each ride — no lock-in.
  • Delete: delete individual rides, or your entire account, from your settings.
  • Control sharing: set per-ride visibility and profile discoverability; manage email notifications.
  • Depending on your location you may have additional rights (access, correction, erasure). Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; we take reasonable measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.
